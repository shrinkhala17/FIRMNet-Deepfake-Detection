About the Project

FIRMNet combines two complementary approaches to expose deepfakes that fool human observers and conventional classifiers.

Spatial Feature Extraction

Our EfficientNetV2-S backbone extracts rich spatial features from the image — capturing textures, edges, and structural patterns that distinguish real faces from AI-generated ones.

FFT-Based Frequency Analysis

The Frequency Attention Gate (FAGate) and Frequency Enhanced Residual (FER) modules operate in the Fourier-transform domain. Deepfakes often leave subtle spectral fingerprints — unusual energy distributions across low, mid, and high frequency bands — that are invisible in pixel space but clear in the spectrum.
